Doors and openings

Front entrance is a double door (1.40 m) into the hall. Internal doors are 0.70–0.80 m; the hall opens to the great room and to Bedroom D through wide openings (1.10–1.20 m) — three of these are the arched openings to be squared off. A verandah door (1.00 m) sits on the east wall of Bedroom D, and a kitchen door (0.90 m) leads out of the back of the house.

Services & condition

Electrics, water, waste runs, bathrooms, ceilings and the cracks to make good. Click any photograph for the full-size version.

The three arches to be squared off

Three internal arched openings become clean rectangular openings. Keep head heights consistent across all three; make good reveals, lintels and finishes.

The stairwell

To become a cooling chimney — the kitchen louvres into it and it exhausts at the top. Rope bannister along the wall, and the door replaced with a lockable security gate. The damp under the stairs needs its source traced, not patched.
